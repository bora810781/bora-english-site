Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

About This App

Bora English Shorts is a personal tool that creates educational videos and uploads them to a YouTube channel authorized by its owner.

Google and YouTube Data

The app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos. Google OAuth authorization is required. The app uses authorization credentials to perform uploads on behalf of the authorized channel owner.

Data Storage

OAuth credentials are stored in private local files and GitHub Actions encrypted secrets. They are not published on this website.

Data Sharing

The app does not sell personal information. Video content is transmitted to YouTube for upload and hosting.

Access and Revocation

Users can revoke the app's Google account access at Google Account Connections .
